## Privacy Policy

**App:** Focus Growing (Android)  
**Developer:** DenizBabat  
**Contact:** [denizzbabat@gmail.com](mailto:denizzbabat@gmail.com)  
**Effective date:** 4 October 2026

This policy explains what information Focus Growing ("the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. We wrote it to match what the app actually does.

### 1. The short version

- Your missions, focus sessions, statistics, world progress, settings and background photos are **stored only on your device**. We have no server and no user accounts, and we never see this content.
- To keep the app free, it shows ads through **Google AdMob**. Premium purchases are handled by **Google Play Billing**. These Google services collect limited technical data such as device identifiers, IP address and interactions with ads.
- Ads appear in two places only: one ad card on the screen shown after you complete a focus session, and optional video ads that you choose to watch to unlock a premium color palette for 24 hours. There are no ads on the timer screen. **Premium removes all ads.**
- **We do not sell your personal data.** We never send your mission titles, notes or photos to anyone.
- In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your choice before any ad is requested, and personalized ads are shown **only with your consent**. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices**.

### 2. Who is responsible

The developer **DenizBabat**, an individual developer based in Türkiye, is the data controller for the processing described here. For any privacy question or request, write to **denizzbabat@gmail.com**.

### 3. Information that stays on your device

The app creates and keeps the following on your phone, in the app's private storage:

| Information | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Missions, checklist steps, priorities, categories | Plan and track your goals |
| Focus sessions (start time, duration, completed or not, pauses) | Timer, streaks, statistics and insights |
| World progress (XP, level) | Grow your world |
| Settings (timer lengths, theme, notification preferences, your optional name) | Personalize the app |
| In-app notification history | Show your recent activity (entries are deleted automatically after 60 days) |
| Background photos you choose | Show them behind the timer |

Insights and statistics are calculated **on your device**. This content is not sent to us or to the services listed below.

**Photos.** When you add a background, you pick it with the Android photo picker, a file picker, or your camera app. The app then only gets access to that one picture, not to your whole gallery. It keeps a reference to the picture or, when needed, a private copy inside the app. Removing a background from the app never deletes the original photo in your gallery. Photos you take with the camera for a background are stored only inside the app, so they are deleted when you remove that background.

### 4. Information collected by third-party services

The app includes software from Google. These services collect data automatically when you use the app and process it under Google's own policies. Data is encrypted in transit.

**Google AdMob (advertising)**
- *Data:* IP address (which may be used to estimate your general location), advertising ID and app set ID, interactions with ads (for example views and taps), and diagnostic information about app and SDK performance.
- *Purposes:* showing ads, measuring ad performance, limiting how often you see an ad, and preventing fraud.
- *Where ads appear:* a single ad card under your results on the "focus completed" screen, and video ads that play only when you tap "Watch a short ad" to unlock a premium color palette for 24 hours. The app never shows ads on the timer screen or during a session.
- *Rewarded video ads:* when you watch one to the end, the app saves the name of the unlocked palette and the time the unlock ends **on your device only**. This is not sent to us.
- *Premium:* Premium users see no ads, and the advertising software is not started for them.
- *Consent and personalization:* in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form from Google (User Messaging Platform) is shown before any ad is requested. Ads are personalized only if you agree; otherwise you see non-personalized ads. Your choice is stored on your device. In other regions ads may be personalized by Google by default; you can turn this off in your Android settings (see Section 11).
- More information: [How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

**Google Play Billing (Premium purchases)**
- Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. **We never receive your card or payment details.**
- The app receives only the purchase status and a purchase token from Google Play, so it can unlock and restore Premium. This information is kept on your device.

More information: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 5. Legal bases

Depending on where you live, we rely on the following:
- **Consent:** personalized advertising in the regions where the consent form is shown. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices; this does not affect processing that took place before.
- **Legitimate interests:** ads that keep the app free, measuring ad performance, security and fraud prevention.
- **Performance of a contract:** providing Premium features you purchased.
- For users in Türkiye, the processing relies on the legal grounds in Articles 5 and 6 of the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

### 6. Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | Tell you when a focus session or break ends, and show the running timer |
| Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) | End sessions exactly on time. Optional; the app still works without it |
| Run at startup | Restore a running session after the phone restarts |
| Vibration | Session alerts and haptic feedback |
| Internet and network state | Needed to load ads and to check Premium purchases with Google Play |
| Advertising ID | Used by AdMob as described above; you can reset or delete it in Android settings |

The app does **not** request access to your contacts, location (GPS), microphone, or your whole photo library. The camera is used only through your own camera app, when you choose to take a photo.

### 7. Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may back up the app's database and settings to your own Google account, where they are encrypted and not accessible to us. **Background photos are excluded from cloud backups.** When you move to a new phone directly (device-to-device transfer), your photos can move with the rest of your data.

### 8. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share it for purposes other than those in this policy. Data is shared only:
- with the Google services listed in Section 4, acting as our service providers or as independent providers under their own terms (for example, for ad personalization);
- if required by law, a court order or a competent authority.

### 9. International transfers

Google may process data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States. These transfers are protected by safeguards required by law, such as standard contractual clauses under the EU GDPR and the safeguards set out in Article 9 of KVKK. Where required, a transfer relies on your explicit consent.

### 10. How long data is kept

- **On your device:** until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. In-app notifications are deleted automatically after 60 days.
- **Rewarded palette unlock:** kept on your device and ignored after 24 hours.
- **AdMob and Google Play:** under Google's retention policies.

### 11. Your rights and choices

- **Ad consent:** where the consent form applies, change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.
- **Personalized ads and advertising ID:** on any phone you can turn off ad personalization, or reset or delete your advertising ID, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (the wording differs between phones).
- **No ads at all:** Premium removes every ad.
- **Delete your local data:** remove content in the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Focus Growing → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.
- **Legal rights:** depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict your personal data, to object, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. Users in Türkiye have the rights listed in Article 11 of KVKK. Users in the EEA/UK can also complain to their local data protection authority, and users in Türkiye to the Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK Kurulu).

To make a request, email **denizzbabat@gmail.com**. Because we do not have accounts, we may not be able to link data to you. Please describe your request. We answer within 30 days.

### 12. Security

Data on your device is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

### 13. Children

Focus Growing is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your country. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data through the app, contact us and we will delete what we can.

### 14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when features change. We will change the effective date at the top. For significant changes, we will also inform you in the app.
